GALAXY MAP EDITOR for Star Wars: Empire at War
==============================================

Edit the galactic conquest maps of Empire at War, Forces of Corruption and their mods (Thrawn's
Revenge, Awakening of the Rebellion and others), make new maps, and export them as a mod you can
upload to the Steam Workshop.


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
- Star Wars: Empire at War - Gold Pack from Steam. The mods you want to edit, subscribed on the Steam
  Workshop or placed in the game's Mods folder.
- Nothing else: no installer, no Python or other programs. Unzip the "Galaxy Map Editor" folder
  anywhere (the Desktop is fine) and double click GalaxyMapEditor.exe inside it. Keep the exe and the
  _internal folder together: the exe needs the files in _internal. The folder can be moved or
  deleted at any time; your maps stay in your Documents folder.

Only one copy runs at a time: opening it again brings the open one to the front.

Windows may warn about an unknown program the first time ("Windows protected your PC"): click
"More info", then "Run anyway". Some antivirus programs are cautious about new programs too.

The editor finds the game through Steam on its own. If the game is installed somewhere it can't find,
it asks for the game's folder (the one holding GameData and corruption). It goes online only to look
up the names of Workshop mods that don't name themselves.

Your maps are kept in Documents\Galaxy Map Editor\Projects; settings and a log of any error in
Documents\Galaxy Map Editor.


CHOOSING WHAT TO EDIT
---------------------
At start the editor lists what it finds: Empire at War, Forces of Corruption, and every mod installed.
Click one to edit its maps. Submods installed for a mod are read with it (their worlds and maps
show up in the editor). If the game isn't found, choose its folder (the one holding GameData and
corruption). "Other Mods" (below the map) goes back to this list.

The editor shows each map as the game's files define it. Some of the base game's maps list only a few
hyperlanes between their worlds (Equal Footing has 15 for 56 worlds); that is how the game made them.


CHOOSING A MAP
--------------
The map button (top left) opens the map list by type (for Thrawn's Revenge: Progressive, Regional,
Historical and Custom): point at a type, then click a map to open it. At the bottom of each list, "New ... map..." makes a
new map from one of them, or an empty one.

Right click a map for:
  Copy from map...   this map's worlds, lanes and owners become another map's.
  Reset to Default   undoes every change to the map.
  Delete             deletes a map you made.


START DATES (mods with dated starts, like Thrawn's Revenge)
-----------
The start date button opens the list of every date the mod knows:
  - Tick a date to offer it in the game. A date the mod has no content for yet is marked; ticking it
    warns you (a campaign there may lack factions or heroes, or fail to start).
  - Click a date to see it. Worlds, lanes and owners belong to the date you are viewing.
  - Right click a date: "Copy from date" copies its worlds, lanes and owners into the date you view.
Historical maps have one fixed date; a new historical map gets the date you give it.


TOOLS
-----
  Select           Click a world to see it in the panel on the right.
  Lanes            Click one world, then another: adds the hyperlane, or removes it if there is one.
                   Click the first world again to let go of it.
  Paint owner      Choose a faction, then click worlds to give them to it.
  Create new planet  (right of the faction box) Click an empty spot to make a new world there. It is put on the date you are
                   viewing; "Add to map" puts it on others.
  Move worlds      (off by default) lets you drag worlds. A world's position is shared by every map,
                   so it moves on all of them. "Restore default positions" puts them all back.

Mouse wheel: zoom. Right or middle button: drag the view. Ctrl+Z: undo. Ctrl+S: save.

  Find incomplete planets  jumps to each world missing a battle map or galaxy map model.
  Menu buttons...          (Thrawn's-style menus) every map button of the game's menu. Drag a map onto
                           another button to move it there (a map already there swaps with it), or
                           double click it to choose a button. Moving one of the mod's own maps asks
                           first: everyone who installs your maps gets the move.
  Excluded Worlds          shows the worlds not on this map (purple: from a submod; orange: imported), to
                           add them.
  Import Excluded planets from mod...  brings the worlds of another installed game or mod (that this one
                           lacks) into Excluded Worlds. Adding one copies its battle maps, model and picture
                           into your map. You are asked to confirm: make sure you have permission to publish
                           another mod's files before you upload.
  Proteus spawns           (mods with Proteus warlords) rings each warlord's worlds at the date you
                           view: gold = taken if that warlord is chosen (crown: its capital and heroes);
                           grey dashed = its world, held by another power.


THE WORLD PANEL
---------------
  - Name, owner (at the date you view), position.
  - Remove from map / Add to map. Delete planet removes the world and everything changed about it from
    this map (a world you created is deleted with its files).
  - Land and space build slots, and the space station level (the highest station the world can have;
    a world that starts with an owner starts with its station at this level).
  - Planet picture: the picture the game shows in the planet view. "Change picture..." takes any
    picture. Worlds sharing a battle map share its picture.
  - Land battles, land and space battle maps, galaxy map model: type a name the game has, pick from
    the list, or "Browse..." for your own file. "Open folder" opens the folder the file is in.
  - Planet texts. Press "Keep text changes" after editing.


NEW PLANETS
-----------
"Create new planet" makes a blank world with a red X. It needs a space battle map (and a land battle
map if it has land battles) and a galaxy map model. Its map names are filled in already: put your map
files with those names in its folder ("Open folder") and the X goes away, or pick the game's own.
Exporting a map with incomplete planets can make the game crash or show errors; you are warned first.


SAVING AND PLAYING
------------------
Save keeps your changes and installs every map you made for that game or mod into the game's Mods
folder (Mods\GalaxyMaps_<name>). The status line shows the launch options to play them; set them in
Steam (game Properties > Launch options), start the game, and start a NEW campaign.


EXPORT AS MOD
-------------
"Export as mod..." writes one map as a complete mod folder with a modinfo.json naming the mods it
needs. Upload that folder to the Steam Workshop as it is, and list the same mods as required items.

Maps made with this editor work together: install several and each keeps its menu button. Planet
texts, new planet names, and changed positions, pictures or battle maps of the mod's own worlds are
kept in files only one mod can replace: with two such mods, one mod's versions win.

Map size: the largest maps known to run well in the unmodified game have about 200 worlds. The editor
warns above that: bigger maps may load slowly, run slowly or crash.

When the mod you edited updates, open your map, Save, and export it again.
